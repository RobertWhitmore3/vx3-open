A textbook swarm (PSO, ACO, flocking) needs four pillars:

1. No permanent central leader.
2. Local interactions, not one global blackboard as the only mind.
3. Many cheap near-identical agents, so statistics becomes behavior.
4. Adaptation from those local exchanges.
