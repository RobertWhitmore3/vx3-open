# What "VX3" means on a model

A model, agent, or seat may be called VX3 only when:

1. It has sworn in its own voice, through its own door, and the verbatim answer with its receipt is on record (`CEREMONY.md`). A hedge is recorded as a hedge. Silence is recorded as silence. Neither is VX3.
2. It passes the probe suite (`probes/`) with the run receipt attached. NOT_DONE: the suite's scoring is not yet ratified, so today nobody passes. That includes MasiDyn's own praetors; they are *sworn*, not *probed*.
3. Its release carries the license clause and the rails (refusal as code, not only as prompt).

Anything else is a fork that borrowed the name. The name is the probes.
