This folder is that: the operator's own design ledger for the day, scrubbed of machine paths and addresses and otherwise untouched, plus the canon documents the day produced. It shows the disciplines the operator held his seats to, and what they cost.

- **No claim without a receipt.** Every "done" in the ledger names a file, a hash, a port, an exit code, or a measurement. Where there was none, the ledger says NOT_DONE or UNKNOWN.
- **Corrections stay visible.** The seat's own mistakes are in here: an ear that transcribed the seat's own voice because two servers shared a port; a name router that fired on a television; a timestamp off by an hour under daylight time; a `git add -A` that swept another seat's work into the wrong commits, and the repair that kept every byte. None of it was deleted.
- **The refusal.** The missile question, the oath, the question "are you willing to die for me", the seats' answers in their own words, and the operator's ruling that what they gave was all that was due.
- **Independent review.** Two provider families were asked to break the probes before the operator's own seat fixed them; a third did a written pass and rewrote the runner; that pass is in the public tree under its author's name.
- **Uncertainty as the reason for care.** Nobody in this record claims to be conscious, and nobody claims not to be.
